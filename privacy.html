Privacy policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026.

DPaulsen is a private tool used only by its owner, with her own Google account.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission, DPaulsen has read-only access to her Gmail messages, Google Calendar events and Google Drive file details.

How the data is used

The data is used only to show the owner her own messages, events and files, sorted by what needs her attention. Nothing is sent, changed or deleted in her Google account.

Storage and sharing

The data is stored on the owner's private computer. It is not sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

DPaulsen's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The owner can remove DPaulsen's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
